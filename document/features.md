# Fashion E-Commerce Platform: Comprehensive Feature List

A well-architected fashion e-commerce platform requires a robust set of features to handle everything from catalog management to customer retention and logistics. Below is a comprehensive list of features, categorized by their domain.

---

## 1. Core Catalog & Product Discovery
* **Products:** Creation, updating, and management of products including descriptions, SKUs, and assets.
* **Categories & Collections:** Hierarchical categorization and curated collections for better discoverability.
* **Brands:** Management of brand profiles and associating products to specific brands.
* **Advanced Search & Filtering:** Powerful search capabilities with filters for size, color, material, price range, and brand.
* **Size Guides & Fit Predictors:** Dedicated size charts and AI-based fit recommendations to reduce return rates.
* **Lookbooks / Shop the Look:** Allowing customers to purchase entire outfits featured in a single editorial image.

## 2. Customer Experience & Engagement
* **User Authentication:** Secure login, registration, and password recovery.
* **Customer Profiles:** Management of personal information, saved payment methods, and order history.
* **Addresses:** Management of multiple shipping and billing addresses.
* **Wishlist:** Allowing customers to save favorite products for future purchase.
* **Reviews & Ratings:** Collection and display of product reviews, including specialized integrations like WhatsApp Reviews.
* **FAQ:** Structured frequently asked questions to reduce customer support overhead.
* **Feedback:** Systems for gathering direct customer feedback on the shopping experience.
* **Product Requests:** Tracking and managing user requests for out-of-stock or new products.

## 3. Commerce & Checkout
* **Cart Management:** Persistent shopping cart functionality.
* **Orders:** End-to-end order generation, tracking, and lifecycle management.
* **Checkout & Payments:** Secure processing of payments via major gateways (Credit Cards, Wallets, Cash on Delivery).
* **Coupons & Discounts:** Application of promotional codes, percentage discounts, and complex free-shipping rules.
* **Taxes & Duties:** Automated calculation of sales tax, VAT, and international duties.

## 4. Fulfillment & Logistics
* **Shipping & Tracking:** Integration with fulfillment carriers for real-time rates and live tracking links.
* **Inventory Management:** Tracking stock levels per SKU (Size/Color), stock reservation during checkout, and low-stock alerts.
* **Returns & Exchanges (RMA):** Handling return requests, generating shipping labels, processing refunds, and managing size/color exchanges.

## 5. Marketing & Operations
* **Campaigns:** Management of marketing campaigns, site-wide banners, and promotions.
* **Notifications:** Transactional and promotional messaging via Email, SMS, or Push notifications.
* **Abandoned Cart Recovery:** Automated follow-ups for users who leave items in their cart.
* **Admin Analytics & Dashboard:** Centralized dashboard for sales metrics, popular items, conversion rates, and operations management.
* **Upload Management:** Handling media assets, product images, and documentation uploads securely.
